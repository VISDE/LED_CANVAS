# Led Canvas v0.2 Alpha Privacy Notice

Status: draft for v0.2 alpha sharing, not legal advice. This document should be
reviewed by counsel before broad public distribution, paid beta, or commercial
launch. It describes the current v0.2 alpha only.

Effective date: `2026-05-18`

Owner/publisher: `VISDE`

Product: `Led Canvas`

Version scope: `0.2.0-alpha`

## Short Version

Led Canvas v0.2 alpha does not include Led Canvas account login, Stripe billing,
device activation, server-issued entitlement, automatic update checks, or
automatic telemetry upload.

The alpha can still involve limited data in these places:

- Website or download host logs, depending on where the page and installer are
  hosted.
- Local installer logs under `<SimHubRoot>\_LedCanvasLogs\`.
- Local plugin settings and SimHub data on your machine.
- Support messages, screenshots, and logs that you choose to send.

## 1. Scope

This notice covers the v0.2 alpha download page, installer, plugin, and voluntary
support interactions. It does not cover future v0.5/v1.0 account, billing,
membership, Discord role sync, device activation, or entitlement systems.

Future account-based releases will need an updated privacy notice before they
collect account, billing, device activation, Discord, Google, support portal, or
download-token data.

## 2. Data Led Canvas Does Not Collect In v0.2

The v0.2 alpha does not intentionally collect or transmit:

- Led Canvas account username or password.
- Stripe customer, payment, card, invoice, refund, or chargeback data.
- Google or Discord OAuth identity.
- Device activation records.
- Hardware fingerprint.
- Server-issued entitlement.
- Automatic analytics events.
- Automatic crash reports.
- Automatic plugin telemetry uploads.

The static page draft does not include analytics scripts, ad tracking, cookies,
or account forms.

## 3. Website And Download Hosting Data

If the v0.2 page or installer is hosted through Cloudflare Pages, Vercel,
Cloudflare R2, S3, GitHub Releases, or another provider, that provider may
process standard request data such as:

- IP address.
- User agent.
- Request URL.
- Referrer, if provided by the browser.
- Timestamp.
- Download status and bandwidth.

Led Canvas should use this data only for security, abuse prevention, download
availability, troubleshooting, and basic release operations. Do not add
advertising pixels or behavioral analytics to the v0.2 page without updating
this notice.

## 4. Local Installer And Plugin Data

The installer and plugin may create or update local files on your machine,
including:

- Installed plugin binaries.
- Led Canvas settings and profile files under the SimHub installation or SimHub
  plugin data folders.
- Installer helper logs under `<SimHubRoot>\_LedCanvasLogs\`.
- Installer state and backup metadata used by the local install path.

These files stay on your machine unless you choose to share them for support or
unless a future version adds explicit server features under an updated notice.

Local logs can include file paths, installer steps, plugin version, SimHub
version, timestamps, and error details. They should not include payment data or
Led Canvas account tokens because v0.2 does not have those systems.

## 5. Support Data You Choose To Send

If you request support or send feedback, you may choose to send:

- Name or handle.
- Email address or tester handle, if you use those channels.
- Screenshots or videos.
- Installer logs or plugin logs.
- SimHub version, Windows version, plugin version, device type, and reproduction
  steps.
- Feature requests or bug reports.

Use support channels carefully. Review logs and screenshots before posting them
publicly. Do not send payment details, private tokens, passwords, unrelated
personal files, or sensitive personal information.

## 6. How Data Is Used

For v0.2 alpha, data is used to:

- Provide the download page and installer file.
- Verify release integrity and troubleshoot failed downloads.
- Diagnose installer and plugin issues.
- Respond to support requests.
- Improve Led Canvas features, compatibility, documentation, and installer
  reliability.
- Protect the download page and release channel from abuse.

## 7. Sharing

Led Canvas may rely on service providers for hosting, downloads, support, or
communications. Those providers process data only as needed to provide their
services.

Led Canvas does not sell v0.2 alpha tester personal information and does not use
v0.2 support data for advertising.

Led Canvas may disclose data if required by law, to protect rights and safety,
to investigate abuse, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable
law.

## 8. Retention

Suggested v0.2 retention baseline:

- Local installer/plugin logs remain on your machine until you delete them.
- Support messages and voluntarily submitted logs should be retained only as
  long as needed for support, product improvement, and reasonable recordkeeping.
- Hosting/download logs should follow the selected provider's retention settings
  and should be minimized where practical.

Before public launch, replace this section with the actual retention settings
for the chosen hosting, download, and support providers.

## 9. Security

Led Canvas should minimize the data it collects, keep generated installers and
release artifacts out of git, avoid collecting secrets in logs, and limit
support access to the data needed to resolve the issue.

No internet transmission or storage system is perfectly secure. Do not send
sensitive unrelated data through alpha support channels.

## 10. Your Choices

For v0.2 alpha:

- You can choose not to download or install the alpha.
- You can delete local installer logs under `<SimHubRoot>\_LedCanvasLogs\`.
- You can choose whether to send logs, screenshots, or support details.
- You can request deletion of support messages or submitted logs where practical
  by using the published support contact.

## 11. Children

Led Canvas is not intended for children. Do not use the alpha if you are not old
enough to consent to software terms and privacy notices in your jurisdiction.

## 12. International Users

Led Canvas may be developed, hosted, or supported from locations different from
where you live. By using the alpha or contacting support, your information may be
processed in those locations, depending on the hosting and support providers
selected for publication.

## 13. Changes

This notice may change as Led Canvas moves from v0.2 alpha to account-based paid
beta or commercial release. Future versions that add Clerk account login, Stripe
billing, Discord/Google connected accounts, device activation, signed
entitlement, support tickets, or download tokens require an updated privacy
notice before those features are used.
